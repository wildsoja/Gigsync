How do I buy tickets?

Pick an event above, choose your tickets, and check out. Your tickets arrive by email right away, each with its own QR code. You can also open them any time from the link on your confirmation page.

How do I get in at the door?

Show the QR code on your phone (or a screenshot or printout). We scan it at the door. Each code works once, so don't share screenshots of your ticket.

Can I get a refund?

There are no returns on Wild Nation tickets, but you can pass your ticket on to someone else.

Didn't get your tickets?

Check your spam folder first. Still nothing? Email us at wildnationgta@gmail.com with your order number and we'll resend them.

Are there age restrictions?

Most of our events are 19+ with valid government ID. Each event page lists its age policy.

Is there parking?

Parking depends on the venue. Check the event page for the address, or contact the venue directly.

Can I reserve a booth or table?

When booths are available they're listed as a ticket type on the event page. For larger groups, send us a message.
